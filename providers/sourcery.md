# Sourcery free tier

🤖 Coding agents & CLIs · no card · provisional — added on 2026-11-03, a regular row from the first probe it passes on or after 2026-11-17 · **live** — last verified by a probe on 2026-11-12 · [sourcery.ai](https://www.sourcery.ai) · [back to the whole list](https://mvalentsev.github.io/awesome-free-ai-coding/)

## What you get

AI pull-request reviews, security checks and suggested fixes for public repositories, with a separate no-card trial for private repositories

## Free models

The vendor does not identify which models the free offer includes. See Limits for the published conditions.

## Limits, in the vendor's words

Public repositories: Reviews allowance: amount not published (period not published); scope not published

Install the GitHub or GitLab integration and grant repository access. Public repositories receive ongoing Free reviews; the numerical allowance and reset are not published. The separate organization trial provides Team features for 14 days without a card, then private-repository use requires a paid plan. Sourcery says it keeps no copy of submitted code. Its model providers do not train on code or messages and may retain them for up to 30 days; zero-retention arrangements require Pro and a separate request.

## Where it is offered

The vendor names no country it keeps the offer from ([source](https://www.sourcery.ai/terms), read 2026-11-03).

## What happens to what you send

What you send is not used to train models. In the vendor's words: “do not use any of your code or messages to train their models” ([source](https://www.sourcery.ai/privacy)).
