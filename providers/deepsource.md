## What you get

AI code review and suggested fixes on existing repositories, with a no-card introductory trial and bundled AI Review credits

## Free models

The free offer is a budget for model usage across the catalog. See Limits for its conditions and Connect for API details, where available.

## Limits, in the vendor's words

Trial AI Review credits: One-time USD allowance: amount not published; scope not published

The trial lasts 14 days and includes up to $50 in AI Review credits; the actual grant is not guaranteed to equal that ceiling. Repository installation and permissions are required. AI Review is separately metered by processed lines of code. The ongoing Open Source plan includes static analysis, but its AI Review is pay-as-you-go. DeepSource does not train on code or data unless you explicitly opt in; its third-party AI providers process code without retaining it. DeepSource account and service data have separate retention policies.

## Where it is offered

## What happens to what you send

What you send is not used to train models. In the vendor's words: “does not use your code or data to train … unless you explicitly opt in” ([source](https://deepsource.com/legal/privacy)).
