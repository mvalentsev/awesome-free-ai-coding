## Limits, in the vendor's words

The $0 plan is "Light quota to code with agents", "Limited model availability", unlimited inline edits and unlimited Tab completions. Which models that quota reaches is published nowhere. "Access to OpenAI, Claude, and Gemini frontier models" is what the $20 Pro plan adds; the only mention of a small model on the whole page is a tip about making a quota last; and the one tier-tagged catalogue Cognition serves, docs.devin.ai/desktop/models, carries TEAMS_TIER_PRO and TEAMS_TIER_ENTERPRISE_SAAS and no free tier at all, pricing Claude Haiku 4.5 under Enterprise. The product was renamed on 2 June 2026 — "Windsurf is now Devin Desktop" — and windsurf.com, windsurf.com/pricing and docs.windsurf.com all 308 to devin.ai, where the word Windsurf no longer appears on the pricing page (read 2026-08-14). That pricing page has since gone behind a Vercel Security Checkpoint — every devin.ai path answers HTTP 429 and "Enable JavaScript to continue", from CI and from a laptop alike — so the probe reads Cognition's docs instead, which are open and still describe the plan: a "daily and weekly usage allowance that refreshes automatically", where Free means "Wait until your next daily or weekly reset" and only the paid plans can buy their way past it (read 2026-08-19)
