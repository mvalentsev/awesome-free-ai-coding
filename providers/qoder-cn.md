# Qoder CN (formerly Lingma) free tier

🎁 Trials (no card when possible) · no card · provisional — added on 2026-09-27, a regular row from the first probe it passes on or after 2026-10-11 · **live** — last verified by a probe on 2026-10-08 · [qoder.cn](https://qoder.cn) · [back to the whole list](https://mvalentsev.github.io/awesome-free-ai-coding/)

## What you get

Alibaba Cloud's agentic coding apps for China — Qoder CN IDE, JetBrains plugin and CLI, formerly Tongyi Lingma — with a two-week trial of 300 credits, then 100 credits a day to claim while that promotion runs, and limited completions on a basic model after

## Free models

No model is free by itself here: the free part is an amount the account spends across the catalog, so the column names none. The limits below say what it buys; the ids to call, where the row has them, are under Connect.

## Limits, in the vendor's words

Pro trial: 300 credits once per account (Valid for 2 weeks)

Claimed promotional credit: 100 credits/day per account (Each claim valid for 30 days)

Basic-model fallback: Completions allowance: amount not published (period not published) per account

After trial credit runs out, a basic model still provides limited completion. The daily promotion must be claimed in the CN desktop app; its end is "结束时间：另行公告" (to be announced). Each claim window runs from 10:00 to the next day at 10:00 Beijing time (UTC+8); unclaimed credit does not accumulate. Signup requires an Alibaba Cloud account bound to a phone number; real-name verification is needed for purchases. International Qoder has a separate company, account and price list.
