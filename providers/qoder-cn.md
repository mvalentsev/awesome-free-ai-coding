## What you get

Alibaba Cloud's agentic coding apps for China — Qoder CN IDE, JetBrains plugin and CLI, formerly Tongyi Lingma — with a two-week trial of 300 credits, then 100 credits a day to claim while that promotion runs, and limited completions on a basic model after

## Free models

No model is free by itself here: the free part is an amount the account spends across the catalog, so the column names none. The limits below say what it buys; the ids to call, where the row has them, are under Connect.

## Limits, in the vendor's words

The free plan, 个人体验版, gives "有限的体验额度，含为期 2 周的试用及 300 Credits" (a limited trial allowance: a two-week trial and 300 Credits) and "有限次数的补全" (a limited number of completions); when the Credits run out "系统自动切换到基础模型，继续提供有限的代码补全等基础能力" (the system switches to a basic model and keeps providing limited code completion). Since 2026-09-18 "Qoder CN 个人用户每天可在 Qoder CN 桌面端内主动领取 100 通用 Credits" (individual users can claim 100 general Credits a day in the Qoder CN desktop app), free users included, each claim "自领取之日起 30 天内有效" (valid for 30 days from the claim), its end "另行公告" (to be announced). An individual account is "手机号绑定的阿里云账号" (an Alibaba Cloud account bound to a phone number); real-name verification is asked only to buy. The same apps outside China are Qoder, a separate company, account and price list. Read 2026-09-27
