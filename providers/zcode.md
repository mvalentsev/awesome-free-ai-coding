## Limits, in the vendor's words

BigModel trial: GLM-5.4: 3,000,000 tokens/day per account per model (First 5 days only)

BigModel trial: GLM-5.3-Flash: 5,000,000 tokens/day per account per model (First 5 days only)

Use the Start Plan in the official app. BigModel publishes separate daily model quotas. Global Z.ai also offers a Start Plan and shows the exact models and allowances after sign-in. Its former GLM-5.3 and GLM-5-Turbo trial allocations are unconfirmed. The app also supports separately funded coding plans and your own API keys.

## Where it is offered

Not offered in Cuba, Iran or North Korea, under comprehensive US embargo, and in no other country the vendor names ([source](https://zcode.z.ai/en/terms), read 2026-09-26). In the vendor's words: “You confirm that you are not located in the following regions: Iran, North Korea, Cuba, Crimea, Donetsk, or Zaporizhzhia”.

## What happens to what you send

What you send is not used to train models. In the vendor's words: “The Optimization Program is turned off by default. Before you actively opt in, we will not use your input information, generated content, or product usage data for product or model training and optimization.” ([source](https://zcode.z.ai/en/privacy)).
