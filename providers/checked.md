# Checked and not listed

137 services whose free tier [the list](https://mvalentsev.github.io/awesome-free-ai-coding/) could not find or could not verify on the date checked. Nothing here is disqualified — domains rejected for cause are in [`blocklist.yaml`](https://github.com/mvalentsev/awesome-free-ai-coding/blob/main/blocklist.yaml) — and each verdict expires after 90 days and is asked again. The records live in [`watchlist.yaml`](https://github.com/mvalentsev/awesome-free-ai-coding/blob/main/watchlist.yaml).
