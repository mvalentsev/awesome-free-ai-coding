# Greptile free tier

🤖 Coding agents & CLIs · no card · provisional — added on 2026-10-31, a regular row from the first probe it passes on or after 2026-11-14 · **live** — last verified by a probe on 2026-10-31 · [greptile.com](https://www.greptile.com) · [back to the whole list](https://mvalentsev.github.io/awesome-free-ai-coding/)

## What you get

AI pull-request reviews on existing repositories with recurring Starter credits; installed CLI reviews use a separate no-card trial or a paid plan

## Free models

The vendor does not identify which models the free offer includes. See Limits for the published conditions.

## Limits, in the vendor's words

Starter: 50 credits/month; scope not published

Startup grant on approval: 500 credits/month; scope not published (Application required; pre-Series A, under $2M revenue in the past 12 months; grant lasts 6 months)

Starter permits one active developer and unlimited repositories. Its credits do not cover CLI reviews; new CLI organizations receive a separate "14-day free trial" with no payment method required. De-identified customer data can be used for AI training; organization admins can turn this off in Data & Privacy.

## Where it is offered

The vendor names no country it keeps the offer from ([source](https://www.greptile.com/terms-of-service), read 2026-10-31).

## What happens to what you send

What you send may be used to train or improve models unless you turn that off. In the vendor's words: “use such data to train and improve artificial intelligence algorithms and models … turn off Help us improve Greptile” ([source](https://www.greptile.com/terms-of-service)).
