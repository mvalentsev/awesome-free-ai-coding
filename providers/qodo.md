## Limits, in the vendor's words

"Free 14 Day Trial no credit card — Unlimited reviews, Unlimited credits" is the whole free offer on the pricing page (read 2026-09-02), and the FAQ beside it says so: "Is there a free plan? We don't offer a permanent free tier. After your trial, pick a paid plan to keep reviewing." The docs put it as "The trial is the initial state of every workspace, not a separate plan or tier" and "When the trial ends, usage stops unless a paid billing setup is active". The plan it trials is "Pro Team $30", "$.012/credit, pooled across the team", "Designed for up to 30 users", "Monthly billing • no commitment". Two caveats the page does not carry: the troubleshooting docs log "above trial max invokes of 30" for an organisation on trial, and the Terms grant Qodo a licence "to train and improve the Platform's, the Qodo Models and Services' performance" while the pricing FAQ says "Qodo does not train models on your code". A standing free lane exists for one audience: "Qualified open source projects can apply to the Qodo for Open Source program for free access", which the Marketplace listing gates at "200+ GitHub stars". The Developer plan the deals list credits it with survives only in the page's metadata. Which models the trial runs on is not stated
