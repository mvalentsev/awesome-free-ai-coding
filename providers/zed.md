# Zed free tier

🎁 Trials (no card when possible) · no card · **live** — last verified by a probe on 2026-10-08 · [zed.dev](https://zed.dev) · [back to the whole list](https://mvalentsev.github.io/awesome-free-ai-coding/)

## What you get

Open-source code editor with a hosted AI agent: a 14-day Pro trial with $5 of model usage and unlimited edit predictions, no card, then 2,000 accepted edit predictions on the $0 Personal plan

## Free models

No model is free by itself here: the free part is an amount the account spends across the catalog, so the column names none. The limits below say what it buys; the ids to call, where the row has them, are under Connect.

## Limits, in the vendor's words

Shared Zed and Delta trial: $5 once per account (Valid for 14 days)

Personal plan: 2,000 edit predictions (period not published) per account

14-day trial: Unmetered edit predictions per account

Verified student plan: $10/month; Unmetered edit predictions per account (Free for one year to verified university students)

No card for the trial. GPT-6 Luna is its only hosted model, and the shared Zed/Delta trial ends when the credit or time runs out, whichever comes first. It then converts to Free. Personal accepted edit predictions have no published period. Verified university students receive a separate year of free Student access. Token credit is a spending wallet at hosted-model prices, not a list of individually zero-priced models.

## Where it is offered

The vendor names no country it keeps the offer from ([source](https://zed.dev/terms), read 2026-09-26).

## What happens to what you send

What you send is not used to train models. In the vendor's words: “Zed does not retain your prompts or code context by default. For Zed-hosted models, Zed has no-training commitments from model providers … Zed only retains AI data when you explicitly share feedback or opt in to training data collection.” ([source](https://zed.dev/docs/ai/privacy-and-security)).
