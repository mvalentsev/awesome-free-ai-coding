# Zed free tier

🎁 Trials (no card when possible) · no card · **live** — last verified by a probe on 2026-11-26 · [zed.dev](https://zed.dev) · [back to the whole list](https://mvalentsev.github.io/awesome-free-ai-coding/)

## What you get

A cardless Pro trial for the Zed coding agent. Existing Student plans continue, and new student applications are paused.

## Free models

The free offer is a budget for model usage across the catalog. See Limits for its conditions and Connect for API details, where available.

## Limits, in the vendor's words

Shared Zed and Delta trial: $5 once; Unmetered edit predictions per account (Valid for 14 days)

Verified student plan: $10/month; Unmetered edit predictions per account (Existing verified students for one year. New applications paused)

The Pro trial uses GPT-6 Luna and ends when its shared Zed and Delta balance or trial period runs out. It converts to Free with your own API keys or external agents. The hosted-model guide says "The Student plan includes GPT-6 Luna and GPT-5.6 Luna only."

## Where it is offered

The vendor names no country it keeps the offer from ([source](https://zed.dev/terms), read 2026-09-26).

## What happens to what you send

What you send is not used to train models. In the vendor's words: “Zed does not retain your prompts or code context by default. For Zed-hosted models, Zed has no-training commitments from model providers … Zed only retains AI data when you explicitly share feedback or opt in to training data collection.” ([source](https://zed.dev/docs/ai/privacy-and-security)).
