## What you get

AI pull-request reviews and repository chat with an ongoing Hobby plan for personal GitHub repositories, alongside a separate no-card Team trial

## Free models

The vendor does not identify which models the free offer includes. See Limits for the published conditions.

## Limits, in the vendor's words

Hobby AI reviews: Reviews allowance: amount not published (period not published); scope not published

Hobby chat: Messages allowance: amount not published (period not published); scope not published

GitHub sign-in and repository permissions are required. Hobby covers personal-account repositories; organization repositories require a paid plan. AI reviews and chat are limited, with no numerical allowance or reset published. The separate Team trial lasts 30 days. AI features are opt-in; Graphite says they do not store or train on submitted data. Repository indexing, account metadata and operational logs have separate storage policies.

## Where it is offered

The vendor names no country it keeps the offer from ([source](https://graphite.com/terms-of-service), read 2026-11-03).

## What happens to what you send

What you send is not used to train models. In the vendor's words: “These features are opt-in and do not store or train on your data.” ([source](https://graphite.com/docs/privacy-and-security)).
