📡 How this list stays fresh

A scheduled job calls every provider twice a week and rewrites this page from what it finds — there is no hand-maintained copy of any figure on it.

Probed, not remembered

Every row anchors on something that dies with the offer: a model id in the vendor's own /models, or wording on the page that states the free tier. A row that fails 3 probes in a row, or goes 60 days without a pass, drops to the Archive.

The vendor's own words

The limits column is the vendor's own words, from the page they were read on. No figure here is a summary of a summary, and a quote that stops matching its source is re-read before it is edited.

Legal free only

Free is by the vendor's own account — a catalog flag, a published price of zero or the vendor's own words on its pricing page. Anything running on pooled consumer accounts, reverse-engineered clients or spoofed apps is refused, and the reason is published with it.
